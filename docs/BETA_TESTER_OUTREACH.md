# Beta Tester Outreach Strategy

A guide to easily finding and engaging new testers for the QAQC Report Generator.

## 🎯 Quick Win: "Build in Public" on LinkedIn
You already have professional looking assets (videos/screenshots). The easiest way to get testers is to show them what you've built.

### LinkedIn Post Template
**Goal:** Show, don't tell. Use the video of the "Results Dashboard" or "Report Export".

> **Subject:** Automating JORC QAQC reporting 📊
>
> I've been working on an open-source tool to automate the tedious part of QAQC reporting. It takes the raw export, processes JORC tables, and spits out a Word doc with all the figures and stats.
> 
> It's specifically built for:
> ✅ Gold Fire Assay, Photon, & Multi-element
> ✅ Duplicate/Replicate analysis
> ✅ Automated Control Charts
>
> I'm looking for a few geologists/DB admins to break it 🔨.
>
> It runs locally (Python/React) so your data stays on your machine.
>
> Comment "beta" if you want to try it out, or check the GitHub link in the comments.
>
> #geology #mining #QAQC #mineralexploration #python #opensource

---

## 🗣 Community Channels
Post in these specific high-value communities. Frame it as "seeking feedback" rather than "marketing".

### 1. Software Underground (Swung)
*   **Where:** Slack Community (channel: `#software-dev` or `#show-and-tell`)
*   **Pitch:** "Hey folks, built a local-first QAQC tool with Python/React. looking for feedback on the statistical methods."

### 2. Reddit
*   **Subreddits:** `r/geologycareers`, `r/mining`, `r/geology`
*   **Title:** "I built a free open-source tool to automate JORC QAQC reports. Looking for feedback."

### 3. GitHub
*   **Action:** Submit your repo to the **[Awesome Open Geoscience](https://github.com/softwareunderground/awesome-open-geoscience)** list. This gets you long-term visibility.

---

## 📧 Direct Outreach Template
Send this to 3-5 former colleagues or contacts who deal with assay data.

**Subject:** Quick question re: QAQC reporting

> Hi [Name],
> 
> Hope you're doing well!
> 
> I'm building a tool to automate improved JORC QAQC charts/tables (so we don't have to fiddle with Excel forever).
> 
> I was wondering if you'd be open to giving it a quick run? I'd love your take on the workflow.
> 
> It's free & open source. Let me know and I'll send the link!
> 
> Best,
> [Your Name]

## 🎁 Incentives (Optional)
If you struggle to get engagement:
- Offer to **build a custom import template** for their specific dataset format if they test it.
- "I'll help you set up your first report."
